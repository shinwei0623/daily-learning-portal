Slowly, her **skill** gets better. When the last ball goes into the basket, everyone smiles. "See? You can do it," her friend says. Mei feels happy and tired. They sit on the floor, drink water, and talk about Saturday's game. Tomorrow they will practice again, and she already wants to help her team win.
