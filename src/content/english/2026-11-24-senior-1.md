Every Wednesday, the school band stays after class for **rehearsal**. Dana tunes her guitar and listens for the **melody** of their new song. The drums start softly, then the whole group joins in. They will **perform** at the autumn fair, so every note matters. When they finish, a few teachers who stopped by clap like a small **audience**. Dana's hands are tired, but the music still seems to **inspire** her. She writes one more line in her notebook: practice with friends can turn a simple tune into something they are proud to share.
