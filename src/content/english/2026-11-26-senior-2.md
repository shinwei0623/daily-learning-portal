Last weekend, our class went to the local wetland for a nature **conservation** project. We wanted to help protect the birds and local plants. As a **volunteer**, I helped clean up the trash near the water. Later, our teacher showed us how to **observe** migrating birds through binoculars. It was amazing to see them so close! We also discussed some smart **innovation** ideas for saving water in our daily lives. The peaceful **atmosphere** of the wetland made everyone feel relaxed and connected to nature. It was truly an unforgettable Saturday!
