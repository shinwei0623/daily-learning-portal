The key to our recipe was finding the right **balance** between sweet and sour flavors. We carefully picked fresh apples, berries, and honey as our main **ingredient** list. After finishing our dish, we sat near the playground to enjoy our meal. We looked out at the distant **horizon**, admiring the **scenic** view of the mountains behind our campus. Everyone cheered because cooking together made our school day super fun and memorable.
